# Research: Complaints and Notifications

- **Decision**: Keep complaint catalogue and SLA targets server-owned.
- **Rationale**: Targets and classifications are operational policy, not screen constants.
- **Alternatives considered**: Client-only complaint definitions were rejected because they become stale.
- **Deferred**: Native push provider is unspecified and not implemented.
- **Sub-types removed**: A second "which part?" choice was replaced by the partner's own title; CRM routes on the category, and every sub-type carried the same target band, so collapsing them changed no promise.
