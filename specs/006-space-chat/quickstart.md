# Quickstart: Space and Chat

1. Run with `--dart-define=DATA_SOURCE=server` (Space otherwise uses the in-memory mock), sign in as each role, and compare role-targeted Space feeds.
2. Heart a post, add a comment, add one reply, and reload the detail.
3. Confirm the mobile app has no post-creation operation.
4. Open partner and department chats, send a message, and confirm opening the thread clears its unread badge.
5. Verify All/Read/Unread filters.
6. Try an unregistered number and your own number; confirm neither opens a conversation.
