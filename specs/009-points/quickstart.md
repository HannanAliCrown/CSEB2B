# Quickstart: Points and Targets

1. Load a user without a scheme and verify ledger visibility plus no-scheme targets state.
2. Load a signed-scheme user and verify four-month/annual progress and extras.
3. Send a valid manual transfer and verify sender/receiver entries.
4. Test disallowed pair, restriction, self, unknown, non-positive amount, and insufficient-point failures.
5. Confirm sent points do not increase the sender's target score.
6. Open the full points ledger and apply credit/debit and date-range filters.
