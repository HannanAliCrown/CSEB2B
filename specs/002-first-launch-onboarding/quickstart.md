# Quickstart: First Launch Onboarding

1. Clear app preferences.
2. Launch the app and dismiss notification permission with Allow and Don't allow in separate runs.
3. Confirm language modal follows both outcomes.
4. Confirm English is selected and remembered by default.
5. Uncheck Remember my choice, finish onboarding, relaunch, and confirm the language modal returns.
6. Deny or skip location and confirm Login remains reachable.
