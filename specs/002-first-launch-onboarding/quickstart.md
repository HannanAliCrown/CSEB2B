# Quickstart: First Launch Onboarding

1. Clear app preferences.
2. Launch the app and dismiss notification permission with Allow and Don't allow in separate runs.
3. Confirm language modal follows both outcomes.
4. Confirm English is selected and Remember my choice is checked by default.
5. Uncheck Remember my choice, finish onboarding, relaunch, and confirm only the language modal returns before Login.
6. Deny location and confirm the switched-off screen offers Open Settings and Continue without location; choose "Not now" in another run. Confirm Login remains reachable in both.
