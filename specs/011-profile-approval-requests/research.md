# Research: Profile Approval Requests

- **Decision**: Keep buying-source approval in the partner service and leave MO/CRM approval to Teams.
- **Rationale**: The mobile partner app only decides the buying-source step.
- **Alternatives considered**: Exposing CNIC data to the buying source was rejected for privacy.
- **Calling**: Pending approver calls open the phone dialer with placeholder `0000000000`; per-approver stored numbers are not yet supplied.
