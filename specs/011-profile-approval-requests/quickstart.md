# Quickstart: Profile Approval Requests

1. Submit or seed an application naming a buying source.
2. Open the buying-source inbox and verify only outstanding assigned requests appear.
3. Open detail and confirm CNIC number/images are absent.
4. Approve without expected purchase and reject without reason; confirm refusal.
5. Approve with expectation and reject with reason; confirm the request leaves the inbox.
6. Verify pending approver call opens the dialer with the stored number.
