# Quickstart: Profile Approval Requests

1. Submit or seed an application naming a buying source (seed `CSE-5560071` names `3007781204` first and `3014429911` second).
2. Open the buying-source inbox and verify only outstanding assigned requests appear; the second source sees none.
3. Open detail and confirm CNIC number/images are absent.
4. Approve without expected purchase and reject without reason; confirm refusal.
5. Approve with expectation and reject with reason; confirm the request leaves the inbox and a rejection ends the application.
6. On the applicant's Approval Status, verify each outstanding approver's Call opens the dialer with placeholder `0000000000`.
