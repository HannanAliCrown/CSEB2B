# Quickstart: Wallet, Cash Requests, and Ledger

1. Seed two accounts and a wallet balance.
2. Send a transfer below PKR 100, to self, to an installer, and above balance; confirm refusal and no state change.
3. Send a valid transfer; confirm sender debit is held and the receiver's ledger is unchanged.
4. Approve one request and reject another from the receiver's Cash Requests inbox; confirm both account balances.
5. Open ledger, apply date/direction/type filters, inspect held running balance, and export CSV.
